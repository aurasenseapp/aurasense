Aurasense — Privacy Policy

Plain language on purpose. Last updated: 22 October 2026.

THE SHORT, HONEST VERSION

  • We collect as little as possible. The safest data is the data we never collect.
  • We never sell your information, and we never show ads.
  • The most personal things stay on your own device and never reach us: your well-being notes, your life story, your inner readings, and your real name and street if you give them.
  • In the Mirror, you choose who listens. With the written rules, nothing leaves your device. If you choose Claude, an AI, your words go to it only to shape each reply: Aurasense keeps nothing, and your words are not used to train AI.
  • Photos you attach to offerings are visible only to you.
  • We never analyse faces or attempt to read emotion from images.
  • You can ask us to delete your kept account and data at any time.
  • Aurasense is for people 18 and over.

The rest of this page explains the detail, as Canadian privacy law asks us to.

1 · Who is responsible

Aurasense is operated by its founder — an independent sole operator based in Alberta, Canada. The founder is the person accountable for your personal information under Canada's PIPEDA (the federal privacy law) and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), and the person who answers any privacy question: the founder (privacy lead), hello@aurasense.app.

2 · What we collect, and why

a) If you use "Keep my light" (optional)

  • Your email address, only so a one-time six-digit code can be sent, and so your aura can return to you on another device.
  • We do not ask for or store a password.
  • Your email is never shown publicly and is never attached to your aurasenses.
  • What travels with your kept light: your Aurasenser name, your home town if you set one, your aura's colours, your Openings, and the choices you make about your senses, personality and interests, so your aura is the same on any device. Nothing more. Your home town is never shown to anyone else.

b) What you offer to the map

  • The senses and words you choose to place, and the location you attach to them. This is stored so the shared map can exist. Offerings on the shared map are public — please don't include private identifying details.
  • Offerings of tender feelings, such as stress, loneliness or heaviness, are softened to about 200 metres before they leave your device, so the exact spot is never saved or sent.
  • To show which town or area an offering is in, its location is looked up with Mapbox, our map provider.
  • A random session number (never your name or email) is kept with what you offer, so you can see your own offerings and take them back.
  • A photo attached to an offering is different: it stays private to you. It exists for your own reflection — so your feeling has something to stand next to — and is never displayed publicly.

c) Kept on your device only — we never receive this

Your Mirror conversations when the written rules answer, your well-being and Aurawellness notes, your life story, your inner readings and what they show, your real name and street if you give them, the mood and spiritual choices you make about yourself, and what you notice after an offering (whether a sense changed, and any words you write about it) all live in your browser's storage on your own device. If you clear your browser data, they are gone — and we cannot recover them, because we never had them.

d) The Mirror, if you choose Claude to listen (optional)

  • Before anything you write leaves the page, the Mirror asks who you would like to listen: Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, or the Mirror's written rules, which run on your own device. Your choice is remembered on your device, and you can change it at any moment from the line beneath the Mirror.
  • If you choose Claude, each thing you write, with the conversation so far, goes through a small Aurasense helper (run on Cloudflare) to Anthropic, only so Claude can shape its reply. Aurasense keeps none of it: not in our database, not in logs, and not on your device.
  • Anthropic deletes it within 30 days. If Anthropic's automatic safety checks flag it as breaking Anthropic's usage rules, Anthropic may keep it for up to 2 years for safety review. Anthropic does not train its AI on it. (How long Anthropic keeps it · Anthropic's commercial terms)
  • Words that clearly say you may be in danger (such as wanting to end your life) are never sent: the Mirror shows you how to reach a real person instead. If Claude hears danger in other words, it points you to real help too.
  • To keep the listening fair and affordable, the helper counts how many replies come from one internet address in a day. It counts with a scrambled code made from the address, never the address itself, kept for about a day and never joined to your words.
  • Claude is an AI, not a person and not a therapist. What it says is a reflection, never advice.

e) Basic technical information

Like most websites, our hosting and infrastructure providers process limited technical data (such as an IP address) to deliver and secure the Service, and our database provider keeps short-term technical records of sign-ins to keep accounts safe. We use this only to run and protect Aurasense — never to build profiles of you. We use no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers; only the essential local storage the Service needs to work.

3 · What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not show advertising or let advertisers target you.
  • We do not rank, score, or profile people.
  • We do not analyse faces or attempt to read emotion from images — ever. What you feel is yours to say, never ours to guess.
  • We never keep or read your Mirror conversations. If you choose Claude, your words pass through to it, and Aurasense keeps none of them.

4 · The services that help us run, and where

Aurasense is a small, independent project built on trusted infrastructure. These providers process data only to make the Service work:

  • Supabase — secure storage for kept lights and shared offerings, and sign-in. Stored in Canada (its Canada Central region). Its email service, which sends the six-digit codes, may process your email address in the United States.
  • Vercel — hosting and delivery of the website. United States and the other countries where its network runs.
  • Mapbox — the map, and looking up the place names of offerings. United States.
  • Cloudflare — routing the site's address, security, hosting the site (Cloudflare Pages), and the small helper that passes your Mirror words to Claude if you choose it. A worldwide network, run from the United States.
  • Anthropic — Claude, the AI that listens in the Mirror, only if you choose it. United States.
  • Ko-fi, with its payment partners (Stripe or PayPal) — only if you choose to support Aurasense. They handle the payment under their own terms, and we never see your card. We see only what Ko-fi shows us about a supporter, such as the name and message you choose to leave. United Kingdom and the United States.
  • jsDelivr — delivers one piece of code the site uses to talk to its database. A worldwide network.

Outside Canada: the providers above may process technical information (such as your IP address), your email address (for the sign-in code), for Mapbox the location of an offering, and for Anthropic your Mirror words if you choose Claude, in the United States and the other countries where they operate, for the purposes named above. While it is there, it is subject to the laws of those countries. To learn more about our policies and practices for service providers outside Canada, or to ask a question about them, write to the founder (privacy lead) at hello@aurasense.app.

5 · How long we keep things

  • Shared offerings remain part of the map until you take them back, or ask us to remove them.
  • Your kept account is kept while you use it. If you ask us to delete it, we remove the account and your kept light within 30 days.
  • Mirror words sent to Claude are not kept by Aurasense at all. Anthropic deletes them within 30 days, as section 2d explains.
  • Technical records are kept by our providers only for short periods, to run and protect the Service.
  • On-device data is controlled entirely by you and stays until you clear it.

6 · Your rights

Under Canadian and Alberta privacy law you can:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct it.
  • Ask us to delete your kept account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent and stop using the Service.

To make any request, email hello@aurasense.app. We'll respond within the timeframe the law requires. You also have the right to contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta if you have concerns.

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, data protection law there gives you rights too: to see, correct and delete your information, to object to how it is used, to take a copy with you, and to withdraw your consent at any time. We rely on what is needed to provide the service you ask for (your kept light and your offerings), and on your consent for anything optional. You can complain to us at hello@aurasense.app, and to your local data protection authority.

7 · Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including access controls on our database. No system is perfectly secure, but Aurasense is designed to hold as little sensitive data as possible — the safest data is the data we never collect. If something ever goes wrong with your information in a way that could seriously harm you, we will tell the privacy commissioner and you, as Canadian law requires.

8 · Age

Aurasense is for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed to anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe someone under 18 has given us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

9 · Changes

If this policy changes meaningfully, we'll update the date above and, where practical, note it in the Service.

10 · Contact

Privacy questions or requests: hello@aurasense.app.
