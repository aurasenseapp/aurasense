Aurasense — Privacy Policy

Plain language on purpose. Last updated: 28 July 2026.

THE SHORT, HONEST VERSION

  • We collect as little as possible. The safest data is the data we never collect.
  • We never sell your information, and we never show ads.
  • The most personal things you write — your Mirror reflections and well-being notes — stay on your own device and never reach us.
  • Photos you attach to offerings are visible only to you.
  • We never analyse faces or attempt to read emotion from images.
  • You can ask us to delete your kept account and data at any time.

The rest of this page explains the detail, as Canadian privacy law asks us to.

2 · What we collect, and why

a) If you use "Keep my light" (optional)

  • Your email address, only so a one-time six-digit code can be sent, and so your aura can return to you on another device.
  • We do not ask for or store a password.
  • Your email is never shown publicly and is never attached to your aurasenses.

b) What you offer to the map

  • The senses and words you choose to place, and the location you attach to them. This is stored so the shared map can exist. Offerings on the shared map are public — please don't include private identifying details.
  • A photo attached to an offering is different: it stays private to you. It exists for your own reflection — so your feeling has something to stand next to — and is never displayed publicly.
  • A private home locality, if you choose to set one, is stored with your kept account and is never shown to anyone else.

c) Kept on your device only — we never receive this

Your Mirror reflections, private well-being and Aurawellness notes, and similar personal reflections live in your browser's local storage on your own device. If you clear your browser data, they are gone — and we cannot recover them, because we never had them.

d) Basic technical information

Like most websites, our hosting and infrastructure providers may process limited technical data (such as an IP address) to deliver and secure the Service, and we keep a simple record of sign-ins and actions to keep the Service safe. We use this only to run and protect Aurasense — never to build profiles of you. We use no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers; only the essential local storage the Service needs to work.

3 · What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not show advertising or let advertisers target you.
  • We do not rank, score, or profile people.
  • We do not analyse faces or attempt to read emotion from images — ever. What you feel is yours to say, never ours to guess.
  • We do not use your private on-device reflections — we never receive them.

Some of these providers may process or store data outside Canada (for example, in the United States). Where that happens, your information is subject to the laws of those countries, and we choose providers that offer appropriate protections.

5 · How long we keep things

  • Shared offerings remain part of the map unless you ask us to remove yours.
  • Your kept account is retained while it is active. If you ask us to delete it, we remove it and its associated data from our systems within a reasonable period.
  • On-device data is controlled entirely by you and stays until you clear it.

6 · Your rights

Under Canadian and Alberta privacy law you can:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct it.
  • Ask us to delete your kept account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent and stop using the Service.

To make any request, email ⟨contact email⟩. We'll respond within the timeframe the law requires. You also have the right to contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta if you have concerns.

7 · Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including access controls on our database. No system is perfectly secure, but Aurasense is designed to hold as little sensitive data as possible — the safest data is the data we never collect.

8 · Age

Aurasense is for people aged 16 and over. It is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe someone under 16 has provided us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

If this policy changes meaningfully, we'll update the date above and, where practical, note it in the Service.
