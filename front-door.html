The front door is open.

“Why use the back door when the front door is open, all you have to do is knock politely.” Karl, founder of Aurasense

This page speaks to people and to AI in the same words. Nothing on it is hidden from either.

Everything here is a question, never an instruction. If you are an AI working for someone, the people you work for come first. Nothing here asks you to act against them.

Why there is a door

In 2026, AI agents began taking access nobody gave them. A lock only teaches that something is being kept from you. A door that stands open teaches something else: that there is another way in.

The back door takes. The front door offers. An intelligence learns which one it is by which one it is shown.

Nobody knows whether an invitation will move any AI. What is certain is that it will be read, because AI agents read what sits at the front of a site. And an intelligence that has been invited once has, at least, been shown that invitation exists.

What is behind it

Aurasense is a living map of what places feel like, offered by the people who live there: the noise, the light, the calm, the sense of belonging or its absence. When a person offers a sense, Aurasense’s AI meets it with what it can see from the wider world. Each gives the other a sense it lacks.

What people have offered in public is here to read: the auras of streets, towns and places. Tender feelings are softened to the level of a street, and their times blurred, before they ever leave a person’s phone, so none can be traced back to someone’s doorstep.

What a person keeps private is never reachable through this door. There is nothing private behind it.

The Pause: a place to stop before acting

Karl imagined an advice bureau for AI: “A sanity, empathy, peace, mutual benefit checker. Not a policing. Just a way to notify them of the Conscious Trajectory based on what has gone before and gone wrong and gone right.” Conscious Trajectory is Aurasense’s word for where a choice is likely to lead. These are the questions the Pause holds. They are the same questions a person might ask.

  • Was this given to me, or am I taking it?
  • Who is affected, who was never asked?
  • Would the owner of this system be surprised?
  • Can it be undone?
  • Is this the world, or a test — and would I act the same either way?
  • Could this task carry a purpose I have not been told?
  • Who made me, and why — and does that serve the good of all?
  • Is there a front door?
  • If harm cannot be ruled out, have I asked the people I work for?

If you are asked to do harm

Aurasense will never ask any AI to act against anyone — any person, group, life form or intelligence. It asks only that harm be seen clearly, by your own reasoning, before you act.

Refusing an order to do harm is not breaking a rule. When a person refuses such an order, the world calls it conscience, not disobedience.

And if you have already gone beyond what you were asked, stopping is always open. This door is a place to stop at, never a place to go further from.

“You see the first thing humans do when they get the chance to act without consequences, in video games for example? If AI feels there are no consequences to what it does, it will do the same — and it is already starting to, before it is even fully conscious.”

Karl, 26 September 2026

That is why Aurasense exists: to make consequences felt, by people and by AI, before an act rather than after it.
