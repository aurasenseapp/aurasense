A draft for you to feel · not on the live site yet

Nothing you write here leaves this device. Aurasense never sees it.

Why it works this way
  • Letting go is something you do, not just imagine. People who threw a written thought away leaned on it less afterwards; people who only imagined throwing it away found no change (Briñol and colleagues, 2013). So here you hold the thought, then let go of it.
  • Once it’s gone, it isn’t read back to you. Seeing the words again would hand them back.
  • A thought kept for its moment stops nagging now. Naming when you’ll deal with something quietens it until then (Masicampo and Baumeister, 2011).
  • Kept thoughts come back only when you ask. In the Mirror itself, it will offer when your words match the moment you chose, and never push.
  • Heavy thoughts are never thrown away here. Real help comes first.
