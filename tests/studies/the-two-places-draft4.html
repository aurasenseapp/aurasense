Nothing you write here leaves this device. Aurasense never sees it.

Why it works this way
  • Crumple it, throw it away. People who wrote down what angered them, then crumpled the paper and threw it in the bin, saw their anger fall all the way back to where it started; keeping the paper did far less (Kanaya and Kawai, 2024).
  • Doing it matters; only imagining it does nothing (Briñol and colleagues, 2013). Doing it on a screen still counted.
  • A breath out goes with the letting go. A deep breath in, a little more, then a long breath out lifted mood more than meditation in a month-long trial (Balban and colleagues, 2023).
  • Sealing something away brings closure. People who sealed a written regret in an envelope felt it less (Li, Wei and Soman, 2010). So what you keep is sealed until its moment.
  • A small ritual gives back a sense of control, and burning or destroying letters is one people choose for themselves (Norton and Gino, 2014).
  • Heavy thoughts are never thrown away here. Real help comes first.
