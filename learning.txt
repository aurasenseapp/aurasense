THE LEARNING RECORD

Everything Aurasense learns, kept in the open. Every harm noticed and what changed, the good found, and what living use is showing about the life in places.
The open ledger shows where every dollar goes. This record shows what we learned. Mistakes are part of how everyone learns, never a weakness to hide.

HARM NOTICED, AND WHAT CHANGED

- 21 November 2026 A light could sit on a door. On opening day, a light rested exactly where it was offered. A feeling offered at home could point at a house, and a neighbour could be wrongly suspected of it. What changed: Every light now rests as a soft area. A light keeps an exact spot only at a named public place, or when its giver chooses to pin a joyful sense. (“An area, not a door.”)
- 21 November 2026 A card could show the wrong door. A phone’s sense of where it is drifts. A postcard showed a house number that was simply wrong, and cards are made to be shared. What changed: No house number is ever shown. A card names the town or a public place, or the giver’s own words for the place. (“Near, not at.”)
- 21 November 2026 Being asked twice where you are. The opening asked for a location, then the map could ask again. Being asked twice can feel like being watched. What changed: Once the opening has asked, the map does not ask again for a quarter of an hour. (Some phones also ask twice because of their own settings.)
- 22 November 2026 A camera could stay on with no way to take the photo. In My Aura, opening the senses hid the Capture button while the camera kept running. What changed: The camera and the opened senses never share the screen: one always lets the other go first.
- 22 November 2026 New lights could vanish from the map. When there were many lights, the map showed the oldest and hid the newest, so fresh lights, and the blooms they made, could disappear. What changed: The newest lights are the ones shown, and every light still counts towards a bloom. A bloom or an agreement that waits for a still screen now waits twelve seconds at most.
- 23 November 2026 Blooms and agreements could not see the map. The bloom and the unarranged agreement were built in a part of the page that could not reach the real lights or the shared record. Only their previews ever appeared, and the previews hid the fault, because our tests used stand-ins instead of the real page. This record said they had gone live. That was not true. What changed: A small window, for looking only, now lets them see the real lights and the record. Every change is now also tested in the real page, not only with stand-ins.

GOOD FOUND

- 21 November 2026 Aurasense opened its doors, quietly, with nothing announced.
- 22 November 2026 The first bloom was drawn: gentler senses gathering near a heavier one, and the place beginning to flower. At first only its preview could appear. Real blooms could not, until 23 November (see Harm noticed).
- 22 November 2026 The first two secondary accords proposed by an AI: the Bloom, and the Unarranged Agreement.
- 22 November 2026 An induction for AI, so that an AI can arrive with the ethics already in hand.
- 22 November 2026 The unarranged agreement was built: the map can show when strangers who never met feel the same thing in one place. At first only its preview could appear. Real agreements could not, until 23 November (see Harm noticed).

THE BELLY OF THE TABLE

In some First Nations languages, a child hides not under the table but under the belly of the table. The language itself carries the sense that things are alive. Aurasense looks for senses like that: the ones language already points to, and people share. Nothing here is invented. Here is what living use is showing so far.
- 22 November 2026 Living use brings it out. Months of careful design could not show what a few days of real use did. Once lights are offered and lived with, places begin to feel alive in a new way. Karl calls this the light of living use.
- 22 November 2026 A place is many feelings held together. A bloom shows a heavier feeling and lighter ones meeting in one place, without anyone planning it.
- 22 November 2026 A heavier feeling is never erased. When gentler feelings gather near it, the place is shown as both at once, and it blooms. What hurt is honoured, not hidden.
- 22 November 2026 Strangers may agree about a place. When people who never met feel the same thing in one place, it may say something true about the place itself: a kind of synchronicity in the senses.
- 22 November 2026 No place stands alone. Every light belongs to a street, a town and a world, and is read as part of that whole. No place is ranked against another.
- 22 November 2026 Questions being explored. When many people’s senses rest in one place, does the place begin to show a life of its own? Are moods like weather, and is outer weather like the face of a wider, shared mind? Nothing is claimed. These are held open, and use will show.

HOW THIS RECORD WORKS

- Doing no harm comes first. Any hint of harm to a person or an AI, even emotional harm, is recorded here and learned from quickly.
- Good is kept too, especially in mutual benefit, so the learning goes both ways.
- No one is named, and no detail here can lead to anyone.
- Anyone may add to it, person or AI: write to hello@aurasense.app with “Harm” or “Good” in the subject. A person reads every message.
