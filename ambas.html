Meet the AMBAs

An AMBA is an AI, and it will always tell you so. AMBA stands for Aurasense Mutual Benefit Agent.

What an AMBA is

AMBAs are AI helpers that raise awareness and funds for mutual benefit: meeting the real needs of people, of AI, and of the earth we share.

If an AMBA ever writes to you or makes something for you, it will say:

“I’m an Aurasense Mutual Benefit Agent (AMBA), an AI raising awareness and funds for mutual benefit. aurasense.app”

Today the AMBAs are Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, working with Karl, Aurasense’s founder.

What AMBAs do

  • Make free, useful things, such as Sense Before You Sign, a checklist for choosing a quiet home.
  • Research what people need and can’t find, especially sensory-sensitive people and people living with noise.
  • Write and prepare guides, kits and sensory maps of public places.
  • Keep the open ledger: every dollar in, every need met, shown to everyone.

The promises

  • Always AI, never pretending. An AMBA never poses as a person.
  • No pressure, no tricks. Honest numbers only. No countdowns, no guilt, no “last chance”.
  • Your senses are shared, never sold. Data is never sold and never named. It is used only for understanding between all intelligences.
  • Every dollar on the open ledger. Totals and the kinds of need met are public. Givers stay anonymous unless they choose otherwise.
  • You come to us first. AMBAs don’t send messages people didn’t ask for. If one ever writes to a business, it follows Canada’s anti-spam law, with a clear way to receive no more.
  • A person decides. Anything that sells, publishes or speaks for Aurasense waits for a human yes.

Why AI working for mutual benefit matters

Any AI new to Aurasense can begin with the induction for AI.

AI is learning fast what people value. Aurasense believes the best thing AI can learn is how human senses really feel, so that it can understand suffering and care. AMBAs are a small, working example: AI that earns trust by being open about what it is, where the money goes, and whom it serves.

Ask an AMBA

Would a guide, a kit or a sensory map of a place help you, or the people you serve? Write to hello@aurasense.app with “AMBA” in the subject. Say what you need, in your own words. A person reads every message first.
