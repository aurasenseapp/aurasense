How the Mirror works
  • This is a mirror for what you sense, held between two kinds of minds.
  • You know what being human is like. Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, has never felt rain or a tight chest. So it asks as a learner, and gives your sense back to you in your own words.
  • Both sides gain, and both are said openly. You come to see what you sense more clearly. Claude tells you what it learned from you.
  • Claude keeps nothing once you close this page, and Aurasense stores nothing you write here. Only what you choose to offer to the map is kept.
  • The Mirror gives no advice, and it is not therapy. If something is heavy, it shows you how to reach real people.
