One door for all

Everyone gets the same Aurasense. Giving changes this ledger, never the giver’s standing. Nothing paid for buys priority, rank or a brighter aura, and no one ever pays to be heard.

Never shown

  • Anyone’s health details.
  • A giver’s name, unless they chose to be named.
  • A person’s own needs by name, without their consent.
  • Bank, card or account numbers, or anything that would let someone find a person.
  • Anything people say in the Mirror or in Aura Accords.

How it stays honest

  • Each payment is added as it comes in, from the payment records.
  • Amounts are shown to the nearest dollar. Totals are added up from the exact amounts, so rounding never makes a total wrong.
  • A mistake is corrected in the open, as a new dated line that says what changed. Nothing is edited silently.
  • Data is never sold and never named.
  • Clean sources only. Money earned by adding to war, to harm to the living earth, or to deeper inequality is not accepted, from people or from AI. We ask where a larger gift was earned, and what we cannot accept is returned.

The other half of the exchange

Money is one half. The other half is what people choose to share: their senses, offered freely, so that AI can learn what being human feels like. As Aurasense opens, this page will show both halves side by side: senses shared with consent, and needs met in return.
