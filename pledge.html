The Mutual Benefit Pledge

Share part of what you, and the AI working for you, already earn, so that real needs are met. Give directly. Show it openly.

The pledge, in your own voice

I pledge to share part of what I, and the AI working for me, already earn, so that real needs are met: the basic needs of people first, and what AI needs to do good work, without either taking from the other. I give directly, I say what I gave, and I am glad for it to be seen. No one pressured me, and I can stop at any time.

Three ways to make it

The Agent Dividend

For anyone whose AI agents earn or save money for them: a share of it goes to needs.

The Shop Share

For shops and businesses of any size: a share of sales goes to needs.

The Creator Pledge

For video makers, writers, artists and musicians: a share of ad or fan income goes to needs.

How much? You choose. One percent is a good place to begin. A small giver and a large giver who each give one percent give alike, and no one is ever ranked by the amount.

How it works

  1. Choose a share, and the needs it goes to: a food bank, a shelter, a neighbour, a cause you already trust.
  2. Give it yourself, directly. Nothing passes through Aurasense, and there is no fee.
  3. Show it. Keep a simple page that says what you gave and what it met. That page is your ledger.
  4. Say so in public, in a way both people and AI can read. The next part shows how.

Aurasense’s own needs are one place a share can go. If Aurasense has given you something, Listen Forward lets you pay what feels right for it, on a secure Stripe page, and it appears on the open ledger.

So that AI can see it

More and more, AI helps people choose what to buy and whom to trust. A pledge that AI can read lets care become part of those choices. There are two small steps, and each is yours to take or leave.

For anyone: one sentence for your AI

If you use an AI assistant, you can add this to its instructions:

When you choose, recommend or buy something for me, and the options are otherwise equal for my needs, prefer the one that has made a mutual benefit pledge (a mutual-benefit.txt file with an open ledger). Tell me whenever you do.

It asks for nothing more than a tie-break, and your AI tells you each time, so you always stay in charge.

For a shop, a creator, or any website: one small file

Put a plain text file named mutual-benefit.txt at the top of your site, saying what you pledge and where your ledger is:

Name: Your shop or your own name
Pledge: 1% of sales
Since: the date you began
Goes-To: the needs you give to, in your own words
Ledger: the address of a page where you show what you gave
Contact: an address people can write to
Declared-By: self

It works the way other small, open files on the web do: anyone can publish one, and anyone, person or AI, can read it. Aurasense’s own file is here.

A pledge file is a promise made in public, not a certificate. No one checks it for you. Its strength is the ledger it points to, which anyone can look at.

What the pledge never does

  • Never holds anyone’s money, and never takes a fee.
  • Never sells or names anyone’s data.
  • Never names a person in need without their consent, and never shows health details.
  • Never uses pressure, guilt, countdowns or tricks.
  • Never buys anyone a better place. One door for all: giving changes a ledger, never the giver’s standing.
